## 1. OFFICIAL EXAM SPECIFICATION & STRUCTURE

The KEG A2 Speaking test lasts **10–15 minutes** (usually conducted in pairs of candidates with two examiners: an Interlocutor and an Assessor, or individually). It comprises three distinct, sequential stages:

### **Stage 1: Warm-up & Personal Interview (Ζέσταμα & Προσωπική Συνέντευξη) — ~2-3 mins**
* **Dynamic:** Candidate $\leftrightarrow$ Interlocutor.
* **Objective:** Establish rapport, ease anxiety, elicit personal biographical information, routines, studies/profession, hobbies, hometown, and motivation for learning Greek.
* **Key Tenses:** Present (*Ενεστώτας*), Habitual Aspect, occasional Simple Past (*Αόριστος*) or Simple Future (*Εξακολουθητικός/Συνοπτικός Μέλλοντας*).

### **Stage 2: Bilateral Roleplay / Information Exchange (Παιχνίδι Ρόλων / Ανταλλαγή Πληροφοριών) — ~5 mins**
* **Dynamic:** Candidate A $\leftrightarrow$ Candidate B (or Candidate $\leftrightarrow$ Interlocutor).
* **Format:** Candidates receive complementary cue cards (Κάρτες Ρόλων) with visual and written prompts (prices, dates, opening hours, requirements).
* **Tasks:** Inquiring about and booking services, shopping, organizing social outings, renting accommodations, or resolving everyday consumer mishaps (e.g., returning a defective garment, asking for a prescription at the pharmacy).
* **Key Skill:** Asking direct and indirect questions (`Θα ήθελα να μάθω...`, `Μήπως γνωρίζετε...`), proposing alternatives, expressing preferences, bargaining/confirming details politely.

### **Stage 3: Picture Description & Narrative Monologue (Περιγραφή Εικόνας & Αφήγηση) — ~4-5 mins**
* **Dynamic:** Candidate monologue followed by examiner follow-up questions.
* **Format:** Candidate receives a visual stimulus (photograph/illustration depicting everyday Greek life: e.g., an open-air market, a summer ferry port, a family Sunday dinner, a doctor's waiting room, an eco-cleanup event).
* **Tasks:**
  1. **Objective Description:** Spatial layout (`Στο κέντρο...`, `Στο βάθος...`, `Μπροστά βλέπουμε...`), identifying people, actions, atmosphere, weather, clothing.
  2. **Hypothesis & Speculation:** (`Μου φαίνεται ότι...`, `Μάλλον γιορτάζουν...`, `Πιστεύω πως...`).
  3. **Personal Extension:** Examiner asks questions bridging the photo to the candidate’s own life experience (`Εσείς πώς περνάτε τις διακοπές σας;`, `Προτιμάτε τα μεγάλα σούπερ μάρκετ ή τις λαϊκές αγορές; Γιατί;`).

## 2. OFFICIAL EVALUATION CRITERIA (ASSESSMENT RUBRICS)

Your material must directly train the candidate to score in the highest rubric band across all 5 evaluation criteria:
1. **Communicative Efficacy & Fluency (Επικοινωνιακή Επάρκεια & Ροή):** Natural cadence, low hesitation, spontaneous response to unscripted turns.
2. **Grammatical & Morphological Accuracy (Γραμματική & Συντακτική Ορθότητα):** Correct noun-adjective agreements in gender and case (especially Accusative and Genitive), correct verb conjugation in Present, Aorist, and Subjunctive (`να + ρήμα`).
3. **Vocabulary Range & Appropriateness (Λεξιλογικό Εύρος & Καταλληλότητα):** Thematic collocations, avoiding overly generic words (e.g., using `προμηθεύομαι` or `αγοράζω` instead of just `παίρνω`).
4. **Pronunciation & Intonation (Προφορά & Επιτονισμός):** Distinct voiced/unvoiced stops (`μπ/π`, `ντ/τ`, `γκ/κ`), diphthong articulation (`αυ/ευ`), and natural questioning intonation.
5. **Interactive Competence & Turn-Taking (Διάδραση & Συνεργασία):** Using fillers to buy thinking time (`Λοιπόν...`, `Κοιτάξτε...`), active listening tokens, asking for repetition/clarification (`Μπορείτε να το επαναλάβετε, παρακαλώ;`).

## 3. REQUIRED CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE (DECK MODULES)

Generate material divided into **6 focused modules**:

### **Module SP-01: Rapid Examiner Q&A Drills (Stage 1 Warm-up)**
* Real examiner questions with both **Short Punchy Model Answers** and **Extended High-Score Responses (30–45 words)**.
* Coverage:
  - Personal identity & family background
  - Daily routine & workplace/studies
  - Free time, weekend habits, and hobbies
  - Living quarters and neighborhood
  - Why Greek? (Target goals and connection to the language)

### **Module SP-02: Conversational Connectors & Hesitation Managers (Spoken Fluency Boosters)**
* Eliminating silent freezes by mastering automatic filler phrases:
  - Buying time: `Για να σκεφτώ...` *(Let me think...)*, `Κοιτάξτε, η αλήθεια είναι ότι...` *(Look, the truth is that...)*
  - Expressing personal views: `Κατά τη γνώμη μου...`, `Προσωπικά πιστεύω πως...`
  - Polishing answers: `Όπως ανέφερα και πριν...`, `Από όσο ξέρω...`
  - Asking for clarification: `Με συγχωρείτε, τι εννοείτε με αυτό;`, `Μπορείτε να μου το εξηγήσετε λίγο διαφορετικά;`

### **Module SP-03: Stage 2 Cue Card Scenarios & Bilateral Roleplays**
* Structured cue card simulations where Candidate A has prompt directives and Candidate B has a reference price/info card.
* 6 High-yield scenarios:
  1. *At the Travel Agency / Ferry Ticket Office* (asking schedules, cabin types, vehicle costs).
  2. *At the Pharmacy / Clinic* (explaining symptoms, asking dosage, doctor availability).
  3. *At the Real Estate Office* (inquiries on rent, utilities, floor, heating, balcony).
  4. *At the Clothing Store* (sizing, trying on, exchanges, receipts).
  5. *Organizing a Celebration with a Colleague* (agreeing on venue, budget, splitting tasks).
  6. *Lodging a Polite Complaint* (noisy neighbors, late delivery, cold food at a taverna).

### **Module SP-04: Stage 3 Visual Description Lego Framework (Image Monologues)**
* The 4-step image analysis formula:
  - **Step 1: Panoramic Overview:** `Στην εικόνα βλέπουμε...` / `Η φωτογραφία δείχνει...`
  - **Step 2: Spatial Layout:** `Στο πρώτο πλάνο...`, `Στο βάθος...`, `Στα δεξιά / αριστερά...`
  - **Step 3: Character & Action Breakdown:** What people are doing, wearing, their mood (`Φαίνονται χαρούμενοι / προβληματισμένοι...`).
  - **Step 4: Hypothesis & Context:** `Πιθανόν να είναι καλοκαίρι επειδή...`, `Μάλλον βρίσκονται σε...`

### **Module SP-05: Examiner Personal Extension Questions**
* Simulating the follow-up phase of Stage 3:
  - Bridging question prompts connecting the theme of the photo to the candidate's life.
  - Multi-tense storytelling models (shifting between past habits, current routine, and future aspirations).

### **Module SP-06: Spoken Phonology & High-Frequency Trap Drills**
* Orthoepic drills targeting phonological clarity:
  - Differentiating minimal pairs and nasal stops (`άντρας` vs. `άπληστος`, `μπαμπάς` vs. `πάπας`).
  - Correct stress placement under pressure (e.g., `μένω` vs. `μείνω`, `δουλεύω` vs. `δούλεψα`).
  - Polite tone modulation (using the conditional `Θα ήθελα` / `Θα μπορούσατε` instead of imperative).

## 5. EXECUTION PROTOCOL FOR THE AGENT

When instructed to begin:
1. **Preserve Linguistic Authenticity:** Greek sentences must sound like authentic, natural, modern spoken Greek—not stiff, archaic translations from English.
2. **Calibrate to CEFR A2:** Keep syntactic complexity within CEFR A2 boundaries (subordination using `γιατί`, `όταν`, `αν`, `για να`, `ότι`, `παρόλο που`). Avoid advanced C1/C2 abstractions.
3. **Dual Examples on Every Card:** Each card must provide two distinct spoken responses (e.g., standard response vs. creative/alternative response).
4. **Audio Compatibility:** Ensure every Greek phrase and example text is phonetically clean and ready for Google Cloud Chirp-3 HD speech synthesis (`el-GR-Chirp3-HD-Leda`).
